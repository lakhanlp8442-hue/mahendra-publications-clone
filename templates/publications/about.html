Aim & Scope

The International Journal of Advanced Science and Engineering (IJASE) published by Mahendra Publications is an open-access, double-blind peer-reviewed international journal dedicated to publishing high-quality original research articles, review papers, and technical notes across science, engineering, and technology.

Covered Disciplines
  • Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
  • Electronics, Communication & Embedded Systems
  • Mechanical, Civil & Manufacturing Engineering
  • Physics, Applied Chemistry & Materials Science
  • Biotechnology, Environmental & Agricultural Sciences
  • Renewable Energy & Nanotechnology
Publication Ethics & Peer-Review Workflow

Every submitted manuscript receives a unique tracking ID, undergoes initial editorial screening, followed by double-blind peer review before final acceptance and DOI assignment.
